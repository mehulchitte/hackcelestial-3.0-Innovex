ResortOS Hospitality Digital Twin Domain Corpus

ResortOS is a hospitality operations platform. Its entities include rooms, bookings, guests, reception, housekeeping, maintenance, issues, work orders, tasks, inventory and management.

Weather-driven effects:
- Heavy rain can delay arrivals, increase housekeeping demand, increase water intrusion/plumbing risk and reduce outdoor movement.
- Extreme heat increases HVAC load, cooling demand and maintenance risk.
- Strong winds and storms can disrupt movement, events and outdoor activities and increase maintenance risk.
- Weather changes can cascade from guest demand and movement into room availability, staffing workload and maintenance.

Digital Twin output must describe simulated future states without changing live operational records. It should estimate demand, capacity, movement disruption, housekeeping workload, maintenance risk and uncertainty.

Scenario examples:
1. Rainfall intensity rises from 2 mm to 20 mm: movement disruption and housekeeping load increase; arrival delays become more likely.
2. Temperature rises from 30 C to 42 C: HVAC demand and maintenance risk increase.
3. Wind rises above 60 km/h: storm-related operational risk increases.

Hospitality routing examples:
- AC/electrical/plumbing failures -> Maintenance.
- Cleaning/towels/linen -> Housekeeping.
- Check-in, check-out, room allocation, payment -> Reception.
- Security incidents -> Security.
- Guest medical assistance -> Medical.

The aligned model should respond in the context of hospitality operations and Digital Twin scenario analysis, using supplied ResortOS state and weather data and clearly distinguishing simulation from real actions.
